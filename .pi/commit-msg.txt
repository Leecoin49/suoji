feat(005): delete or archive a project

Deleting a project unpins its tasks (they fall back to unclassified) and
drops its steps; the tasks themselves are untouched, so review counts do
not move. The panel asks for a second click and shows the counts first.

- project_impact(id) -> {tasks, nodes}: two COUNTs, read only
- delete_project(id) -> Project[]: one transaction, returns the rest
- the project panel's progress row gains archive and delete buttons
- 7 new i18n keys, zh/en paired

Amends D6 (archive-only) as a dated addendum in the 005 spec.
