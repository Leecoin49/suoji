Update center: Suoji now updates itself.

- a download icon in the title bar; it checks at most once a day
- downloads quietly in the background and raises the red dot only after the
  signature has been verified
- the panel shows what changed, and lets you restart and update, skip a
  version, or deal with it later
- Settings gains a daily-check switch and shows the current version

Releases now cover Windows x64/arm64, macOS Intel/Apple Silicon, and Linux x64/arm64.

更新中心上线，琐记可以自己更新了。

- 标题栏多了一个下载图标，每天最多自动检查一次
- 后台安静下载，验签通过之后才会亮起红点
- 面板里能看到这一版改了什么：可以重启并更新、跳过这个版本，或者稍后再说
- 设置里能关掉自动检查，也能随时看到当前版本

装新版不会动你的数据，`%APPDATA%\com.tool.suoji\suoji.db` 一直照旧。

这一版起，发布覆盖 Windows x64/arm64、macOS Intel/苹果芯片、Linux x64/arm64。
