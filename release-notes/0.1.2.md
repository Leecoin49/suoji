Look back on any stretch, and see where the work went.

- the review panel now opens on a conclusion sentence: how many finished, how it
  compares with the stretch before, busiest day, longest quiet gap
- day ranges get a calendar heat map, week and month ranges keep the bar chart;
  hover or focus any cell for the exact date and count
- four metric cards side by side: daily average, streak, comparison, open backlog
- projects: one level, archive instead of delete; tags are free text, up to ten
  per task, de-duplicated by name
- a step-by-step flow per project: tick a step and the next one becomes current,
  rename or insert steps at any time
- everything above is additive: your existing tasks, dates and notes are
  untouched, and the database upgrades itself in one transaction with a copy
  saved next to it first

No new dependencies, nothing leaves your machine.

复盘真正能看出东西来，也能看出事都花在哪儿了。

- 复盘面板开头先给一句结论：这段时间做完多少、比上一段多还是少、最忙是哪天、
  中间断档多少天
- 按天看是日历热力图，按周按月还是柱图；鼠标悬停或者键盘聚焦都能看到那一天的
  具体日期和件数
- 四张指标卡并排：平均每天、连续天数、环比、此刻积压
- 项目：只有一层，能归档不删；标签是自由文本，一个任务最多十个，按名字去重
- 每个项目可以排一条步骤链：勾掉一步，下一步自动变成「当前」，随时能改名或插步骤
- 以上全是叠加：原有的任务、日期、备注一个字没动；升级在一个事务里做完，
  动手前会先把库复制一份放在旁边

没有新增依赖，数据不出本机。
