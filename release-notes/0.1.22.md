Steps inside a project, a deadline that means something, and a font you can read.

- a project now holds a chain of steps, and each step holds its own tasks: rename
  or delete a step, file a task into one, or leave it unfiled - the tree shows
  where everything sits
- the project overview offers three ways to look at the same thing: a fishbone of
  the chain, progress bars per step, or a flat list of everything still open;
  clicking a bone or a bar drops that step's remaining lines underneath
- the home list groups today's tasks by project and step, and collapses; click the
  project name to jump into its panel
- deadlines, not just times: set a date and hour, and the row tells you "overdue 7
  days", "due today 18:00" or "in 3 days" in the colour that matches
- reschedule a task from its right-click menu instead of dragging it around
- while capturing, pick a step straight from the project chip - or create the step
  in place; the "no project" slot is simply called personal
- repeating tasks: daily, chosen weekdays, or a day of the month; each matching day
  creates its own line, and a day you skip quietly expires
- appearance: four text sizes and three typefaces, plus softer-but-deeper greys so
  the secondary text stops disappearing
- every panel now shares one frame - same title, same close button with a word on
  it - and switching tabs closes whatever was open

项目里可以有步骤，截止日期终于有用，字也看得清了。

- 项目下面是一条步骤链，每一步挂自己的事：步骤可改名、可删除，事可以归到某一步，
  也可以先不归；树形里一眼看出每件事在哪
- 项目总览有三种看法：鱼骨看链条结构、进度条看每步走了多少、清单只看还没做的；
  点鱼骨或进度条，这一步剩下的事就摊在下面
- 首页按项目、步骤分组，可折叠；点项目名直接进它的面板
- 真正的截止日期：填日期加时刻，行上会写「逾期 7 天」「今天 18:00 截止」「还有 3 天」，
  颜色也跟着变
- 右击任务可以改期，不用再拖来拖去
- 录入时点项目胶囊就能选到具体步骤，也能当场新建步骤；「不归项目」改叫「个人」
- 周期任务：每天、指定周几、每月某天；到日子自动生成一条，跳过的那天自动过期
- 外观：四档字号、三种字体，另外把次要灰调深了一档，不再糊成一片
- 所有浮层换成同一套框架——同样的标题、同样带文字的关闭按钮；切标签会顺手把浮层收掉
